About this website

This is the personal portfolio of Brad Josh Nebreja. It presents my experience, projects, skills, credentials and CV, and provides a way to contact me about professional opportunities.

When you contact me

The contact form asks for your name, email address and message. These details are sent through Web3Forms for delivery to my email inbox so I can read and respond to your inquiry. Please do not include passwords, payment details or confidential client information.

Web3Forms processes submissions and technical information under its own Privacy Policy, including its retention and spam-prevention practices. Your correspondence may also be handled by my email provider. I use messages for responding to inquiries and relevant professional follow-up, not to publish them on the portfolio.

Appearance

This portfolio uses a dark appearance and does not store an appearance preference.

Hosting and external services

The portfolio is hosted on Vercel. Hosting and delivery services may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs, to deliver and protect the site. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice for its practices.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Checkpoint, CinemaBuddy and other external destinations are governed by those sites’ own policies. Opening a CV or project image downloads the requested file. This portfolio does not operate advertising campaigns or sell your contact information.

Questions and requests

Contact bradjosh.nebreja05@gmail.com with a privacy question or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of correspondence you sent me. Include enough information to identify the relevant message without sending sensitive documents. I will review requests in light of applicable requirements and any legitimate need to retain relevant correspondence.

Updates
